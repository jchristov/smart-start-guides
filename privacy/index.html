Privacy Policy

How Smart Start handles personal data on this website and when you download or purchase our city guides. Effective date: 2026-11-25.

1. Controller & Contact

This website is operated by Smart Start (publisher of the Smart Start City Guides). For any privacy or data protection questions, or to exercise your GDPR rights, contact us at smartstart.digitalcreations@gmail.com.

2. What Data This Website Collects

This catalog website is a static informational site. We do not set tracking or advertising cookies, do not run third-party ad pixels, do not use local analytics trackers, and do not have user accounts or forms on this domain.

  • Static hosting & server logs: This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (smartstartpub.pages.dev). When you load a page, Cloudflare processes standard technical request data (such as IP address, timestamp, requested URL, and browser user agent) to deliver the site securely and prevent abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in secure, reliable hosting).
  • Web fonts: Pages load typography stylesheets from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which transmits your IP address to Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC to serve the font files (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Purchases, Free Downloads & Gumroad

When you click a download or purchase button, you are directed to our storefront on Gumroad (smartstartpub.gumroad.com). Gumroad acts as the merchant of record and processes your email address, payment details, billing country, and download history in accordance with Gumroad's Privacy Policy (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract; Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — tax and accounting obligations).

Links from this site include non-personal referral parameters (such as utm_source and utm_campaign) so we can see which pages help readers find our guides without identifying individual visitors.

4. Email Communications

If you download a free guide, purchase a full guide, or subscribe on Gumroad, we may receive your email address via Gumroad to deliver your PDF, send edition updates, or share reader offers. You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to smartstart.digitalcreations@gmail.com (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Direct Emails & Support

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message solely to reply to your inquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) / Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete correspondence once it is no longer needed, subject to statutory retention obligations.

6. International Data Transfers

Our hosting and storefront providers (Cloudflare, Google, and Gumroad) may process data in the United States or other countries outside the EEA under recognized safeguards such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 45–46 GDPR).

7. Your Rights Under the GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17 GDPR);
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR) and receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing (Art. 21 GDPR);
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing (Art. 7(3) GDPR);
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

To exercise any of these rights, email smartstart.digitalcreations@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our hosting, tools, or legal requirements change. The latest version is always published at https://jchristov.github.io/smart-start-guides/privacy/.
